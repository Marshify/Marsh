# Grand Line First Sea chart

Grand_Line_First_Sea_Refined_Chart.png is the full 2880 × 1536 preview.

- The 14 island sprites use vanilla Minecraft Java 1.20.1 textures and block geometry. Their filenames and world coordinates are in first_sea_islands.json.
- The pirate frame, edge clouds, and the two doubloon sprites are separate assets. The sea and grid are in first_sea_background.png.
- Draw shorelines_overlay.png at full chart size after the surface islands. It is aligned to their positions in the preview.
- palace_submerged.png is the underwater rendering of Lotus Palace. Use it at the same scale as the base sprite and 13 chart units lower in the 1440 × 768 logical layout. Draw underwater_surface.png at full chart size above the submerged city.
- The smoke sprite is centered at (156, 542) in the 1440 × 768 logical layout and fits within 91 × 45 chart units.
- Hollow Islet, the quest badge, the player marker, and the heading cone are omitted.
