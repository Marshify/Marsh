# Saltmarket Docks, rebuilt (0.40.0)

A fortified pirate harbour town, built from `docs/PIRATE_HARBOR_BLUEPRINT.md` in vanilla blocks
(`world/plan/SaltmarketDocks.java`). Island-local coordinates: +x east, +z south (toward the harbour). About 220 x 225.

| Area | What is there |
|---|---|
| Harbour (south) | Promenade at y 64 with lamps, cargo, benches and bollards. Pink-brick harbour wall (bricks softened with granite) up to a wall walk at y 79 with battlements, cannons and blue banners. Nine arches with pale quartz borders: eight cargo alcoves and, in the middle, the **gateway** with steps up into town. Ladders up to the wall walk at both ends. Three piers (P1 x -20, P2 x 12 with a side deck and a crane, P3 x 44) with T-heads. |
| Lighthouse (west) | On its own rock across a channel, reached by a stone causeway from the west sea gate. Dark stone base, white tapering shaft (calcite with white bands and dark trim rings), narrow blue windows, a ladder inside all the way up, a balcony with dark railings at y 113, a glass lantern room and a pointed blue cone; about 64 tall. |
| Town (middle, y 67) | Walled on all sides (corner bastions, west sea gate, landward gate with two towers). **Central hall** (x -14..14, z -24..-4): two timber-framed storeys, orange roof, railed roof terrace with a flagpole, veranda on the plaza. **Plaza** (x -20..20, z 3..31): four planted trees, a well, benches, lamp posts and pennant strings; a road to the harbour gate. **Windmill** (-40, 4): 9 x 9 body narrowing to 7 x 7, blue roof, four open-lattice blue sails. **Green tower** (52, -20): five floors, ladder, steep green spire. **18 houses**, each with its own footprint, storeys, roof colour (orange or green) and ridge, X-braced timber framing, blue windows, open doors, ladders between floors and furniture; extras include chimneys, sheds, awnings, a cross wing and barrels. |
| Interiors | Hall ground floor: the tavern (raised end, posts, round tables and stools, rugs, an L-shaped counter under a red canopy, shelves and barrels, blue chains with lanterns). Upper floor: the kitchen (black and white checkerboard, brick-based island, brick oven with furnace and smoker, cupboards, banners), reached by a ladder. |
| Wilderness (north) | Rolling grassland on a rocky coast with three headlands. Clearing A (-45, -95): the basic camp. Clearing B (42, -100): the stronger camp, with the boss's ring of standing stones and black banner at (46, -112). Dirt paths from the landward gate, boulder clusters and 22 broad dark-canopy trees. |

**NPCs:** quest giver (0, 68, 6) and innkeeper (10, 68, 3) on the plaza by the hall; fruit dealer (-16, 68, 18); fisherman at the end of pier P2;
players arrive on the promenade (12, 65, 60). The Trial Keeper is placed near the quest giver, as before.

**Treasure chests (outdoor only):** legendary on the lighthouse balcony; rare in the boss ring; commons by cargo on the promenade, in the back
alley by the landward wall, and among the rocks on the east headland.

**Older worlds** keep the original Saltmarket (layout version 10; `FirstSeaPlans.SaltmarketDocksLegacy`) and its original chest spots.

**Differences from the blueprint:** vanilla blocks throughout (muted pink = bricks with granite; X-braces of dark oak planks rather than thin
custom models; sails from blue concrete); the lighthouse sits at (-100, 42) beside the west sea gate so the causeway is short; nine harbour arches
instead of eleven; static sails (no rotation yet).
